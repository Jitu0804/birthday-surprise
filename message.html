Our Story

2018 to 2026

A story that still lives in my heart

Was this friendship? Brother-sister? Lovers? Fiancée? Or something else entirely? Honestly, I still feel that we were just two unknown people who looked at each other, and in that one moment not only our eyes, but our hearts, minds, and even our steps stopped right there.

It all goes back to 2018, when I saw you for the very first time in our village in Rajasthan. To be honest, the moment I saw you, everything went quiet. You looked straight into my eyes, and I thought, “Who is she? I have never seen such an apsara in the village before!” That day, something rang inside my heart, and I knew I wanted to talk to you.

Within a few days, we started talking. Every time I spoke with you, my heartbeat would rise, and inside I would feel a happiness that I still cannot explain properly. Your presence made me feel so light, so energetic, so alive. But maybe fate wanted to test us — because you came to Baroda, and our little chats were left behind within the four walls of that village. No contact, no meetings, no recognition. Whenever I went to the village, you were in Baroda; and whenever you went there, I was in Baroda. Our timing never seemed to support us.

But maybe God had decided that our incomplete story should not stay trapped inside those four walls forever. In 2020, we met again in the village at Kaka’s house. The happiness of that day had no limits — it felt like I had found a lost diamond again. I felt as if God had given me one more chance to come close to you and impress you.

During the 2020 lockdown, I put in so much effort to impress you. We played games together, watched TV shows, listened to songs, and such a beautiful vibe began growing between us. You could make me sit or stand with just one look from your eyes! I still remember telling you how cute your expressions were — and even today, that same face and those same eyes can still drive me crazy. I do not know why, but my heart always gravitates toward your face.

As time passed, the distance between us slowly reduced. Holding hands while watching songs and shows, stealing those little moments, talking through risks — we were the ones taking risks right in front of everyone. And eventually, you were impressed... and you fell in love with me.

Even today, I ask myself sometimes: Did we do the right thing back then? When you fell in love, did I truly feel love or was it just romance? In those days, both feelings looked the same to me, and I have carried that guilt for a long time. I have told you this many times too.

Then came 2021, 2022, and 2023. Our conversations happened, but many times it felt like they were one-sided. You put in so much effort, and I stayed careless. You tolerated so much and still stayed. You knew that maybe I was immature and selfish, and yet your wish was so simple — you only wanted me to talk to you with love.

And still, whenever you talked about leaving, I swear the ground would slip from under my feet. My head would spin, my heart would get restless, and I would panic from inside: “Will you really leave me? Why?” In those moments, I would realize how much tension I feel whenever the thought of losing you comes near — and that is when so much love for you would rise in my heart all at once.

“Some stories are never defined by a single name... they are simply felt, deeply and forever.” 💗
